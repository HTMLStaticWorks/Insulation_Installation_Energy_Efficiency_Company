UNDERSTANDING INSULATION

The Science of Savings

Key concepts to help you understand how your building uses and loses energy.

The Power of R-Value

R-Value measures how well an insulation material resists the conductive flow of heat. The higher the R-Value, the greater the insulating power.

Depending on your local climate zone, upgrading your attic from R-19 to R-60 can dramatically shift your building's energy profile, turning a drafty structure into a thermal fortress.

Return on Investment

Insulation is one of the few home improvements that actually pays for itself. Most professional installations achieve full ROI within 3 to 5 years through reduced utility bills.
