FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Have Questions? We Have Answers.

Insulation acts as a thermal barrier, slowing the transfer of heat. In winter, it keeps the heat generated by your HVAC system inside. In summer, it prevents the sun's radiant heat from entering. This means your HVAC system runs less often, directly lowering your energy consumption.

Common signs include uneven temperatures between rooms, high energy bills, drafts in the upper floors, and rapid snow melt on your roof during winter (ice dams). A visual inspection showing insulation below or just at the floor joists is also a clear indicator.

Spray foam is a liquid chemical that expands into a solid foam upon application. It provides both high thermal resistance (R-value) and acts as a complete air and moisture barrier. It is ideal for hard-to-reach areas and complete envelope sealing.

Yes. Exterior walls make up a large surface area of your home. If they are poorly insulated, you lose significant energy laterally. We can often install blown-in insulation into existing wall cavities without major demolition.

Most standard residential attic installations can be completed in a single day. More complex projects, like whole-house wall injections or extensive spray foam applications, may take 2-4 days.

Absolutely. Beyond energy savings, proper insulation eliminates cold spots, reduces drafts, helps maintain a consistent temperature throughout the house, and can significantly dampen outside noise.
