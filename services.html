Attic Insulation

Heat naturally rises, making the attic the primary escape route for your heating in winter and the entry point for radiant heat in summer. Proper attic insulation creates a thermal barrier that dramatically improves whole-house comfort.

BUILDING DIAGNOSTICS

Where Energy Escapes

Hover over the markers to see common thermal failure points.

The Attic

Issue: Up to 25% of heat loss occurs here as warm air rises.
Solution: Blown-in fiberglass or cellulose insulation.

Exterior Walls

Issue: Uninsulated cavities allow direct thermal transfer.
Solution: Dense-pack injection insulation.

Windows & Doors

Issue: Gaps around frames cause significant drafts.
Solution: Targeted air sealing and weatherstripping.

Crawlspace / Floor

Issue: Cold air infiltration from below creates cold floors.
Solution: Spray foam encapsulation.
