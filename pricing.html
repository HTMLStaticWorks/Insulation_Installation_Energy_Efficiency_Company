Material Quality (Approx. 45%)

Premium materials like closed-cell spray foam cost more upfront but offer superior R-value and longevity compared to basic fiberglass batts.

Labor & Complexity (Approx. 35%)

Hard-to-reach areas, vaulted ceilings, and retrofitting older homes require more time and specialized equipment.

Prep & Cleanup (Approx. 15%)

Removing old, damaged insulation and thoroughly sealing the area before new installation.

Permits & Fees (Approx. 5%)

Local building permits and disposal fees for removed materials.
